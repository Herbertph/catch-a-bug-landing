Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This page explains what personal data Don't Break in Public collects, why, who processes it and how you can control it.

Who is responsible

Herbert Parasio, an individual based in Brazil, operating as Unflaky, Quality Lab, is responsible for your data. Contact: herbert.parasio@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you take the diagnostic and submit your email, we store your email address, your score, your result band and the areas that came out uncovered or partial.

When you buy, the payment is processed by Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment details. We receive your name, email address, country and order details, never your full card number.

Like any website, our hosting provider may record technical data such as your IP address and browser in server logs. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address.

Why we use it

To send you emails about your diagnostic, the ebooks and software quality content related to your result, based on the consent you give when you submit your email. You can withdraw it at any time.

To deliver the ebooks you bought and to handle support and refunds, which is necessary to fulfil your purchase.

We do not sell your data and we do not share your list entry with anyone for their own marketing.

Who processes it

MailerLite stores the email list and sends our emails. Paddle processes payments, taxes, invoices and refunds. Vercel hosts this website. Google Fonts serves the page fonts. Each of them has its own privacy policy, and some of them may process data outside your country.

Cookies

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The checkout, provided by Paddle, may use cookies that are needed to process your payment.

How long we keep it

Diagnostic data is kept while you stay subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop sending you emails and you can ask us to delete your data. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in Europe and the LGPD in Brazil, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its use, and withdraw your consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can write to herbert.parasio@gmail.com for anything else. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
